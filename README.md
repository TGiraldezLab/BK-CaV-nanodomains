## BK–CaV nanodomains

Python scripts used for STORM image analysis in the study:

**Kshatri, Romero et al. (2026).** *Auxiliary BKβ4 subunits promote nanoscale coupling between BK and voltage-gated calcium channels.* Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS).

## Included scripts

- `nnd_STORM_v1.py` — Nearest-neighbor distance (NND) analysis of STORM localization data.
- `STORM_cluster_area_v3_1_modif David.py` — Analysis of STORM cluster areas.
- `Copilot_Cluster Analysis_heteroclusters_v5.py` — Analysis of heteroclusters in STORM data.

These files represent the analysis scripts used in the PNAS study and are preserved in their original form. They are provided for transparency and reproducibility; they have not been repackaged as a standalone software application.
